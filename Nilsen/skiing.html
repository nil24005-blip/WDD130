Skiing & Snowboarding in Idaho

Enjoy Idaho's mountains and make the most of winter.

Enjoy Idaho's Winter Mountains

Idaho winters create great opportunities to spend time outside. Skiing and snowboarding are fun ways to enjoy the mountains, get exercise, and spend time with friends. Whether you are learning the basics or already feel comfortable on the slopes, planning ahead can make your day better.

Plan Your Mountain Day

Before leaving, check the mountain's current weather, road conditions, and lift information. Dress in layers, wear a helmet, bring water, and make sure your equipment fits correctly. Conditions can change quickly, so stay aware of the weather and follow posted mountain signs and resort instructions.

What to Bring

Make sure you are prepared before heading to the mountain.

  • Skis or snowboard with properly fitted boots and bindings
  • Helmet and goggles
  • Warm layers, gloves, and waterproof outerwear
  • Water and snacks
  • Phone, charged battery, and a plan for meeting your group

Respect the Mountain

Know your ability level, stay in control, look uphill before entering a trail, and give other people room. Stay on open marked runs and follow all resort safety rules. A good mountain day is one where everyone gets home safely.

Prepare well, ride within your ability, and enjoy Idaho's winter scenery.
